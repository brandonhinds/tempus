One-time data upgrade

Your records stay intact.

Tempus needs to reconcile older sheet layouts before financial pages can open. The process is resumable and begins by creating and verifying a complete spreadsheet backup.

What happens

  1. A dated copy is created in the same Drive folder and checked sheet by sheet.
  2. Columns are matched by header name; populated columns are never silently relabelled.
  3. Legacy company expenses and invoices move into auditable ledgers. Superseded rows are archived.
  4. Expenses switch to a simple paid date. Part payments, reconciliation and void go: paid expenses keep their payment date, unpaid ones stay unpaid, and void ones are removed (a skipped schedule payment stays skipped).
  5. If anything interrupts the run, Tempus resumes at the unfinished migration.
