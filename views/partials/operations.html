Upgrade and backups

Tempus disables financial pages until the one-time upgrade has created and verified a spreadsheet copy. Failed migrations show their error and resume from the unfinished step.

Expenses: recurring bills
  1. Open Expenses → Schedules and choose New schedule.
  2. Enter the vendor, the amount each time (including GST), the frequency and the start date, which is the first payment. Add an end date only if the bill stops. A bill on the 29th, 30th or 31st falls on the last day of a shorter month, then returns to its usual day.
  3. Choose Save schedule. Its payments appear under Transactions → Due and upcoming: this month's, by default, or as set by List scheduled expenses in Settings.

The End date is what stops a schedule: every unpaid payment after it is removed, including Due ones and ones you edited. Ends today fills it in for you. When saving would update or remove unpaid payments, Tempus lists them first, and Due or hand-edited payments keep their amount unless you tick the box. Paid payments never change. New payments are created whenever Tempus opens, including any due since it was last opened.

Expenses: recording and paying

A scheduled bill: choose Mark paid when it's paid. Edit changes this one payment only, and Skip sets it aside if it won't happen this time. Skipped payments are listed under Skipped at the bottom, where Restore brings one back.

A one-off purchase: choose Record expense. It's marked Paid today unless you change the date. Turn Paid off for a bill you'll pay later; it shows as Unpaid until you choose Mark paid.

Cash basis counts an expense on its paid date. Accrual basis counts it on the earlier of its invoice date and paid date. A taxable expense gives its GST credit (scaled by business use) in the same period.

Fixing mistakes: you can always Edit or Delete an expense. If the change alters a BAS you've marked as submitted, Tempus warns you first.

Expenses and GST

On a cash basis an expense counts when it is paid, so an unpaid expense counts as $0. On an accrual basis it counts on the earlier of its invoice date and paid date. A taxable expense gives its GST credit (BAS 1B) in that same period, scaled by its business use; GST-free, input-taxed and out-of-scope expenses give none. No receipt or reconciliation is needed. Upcoming schedule payments are forecasts, never actuals. The dashboard, the BAS table and the BAS calculation all read company expenses from the Expenses page. The Deductions page is for salary sacrifice and extra super only.

Assessments

Turn on Lil Assessments mode to open Assessments at the current month. Record the client, date of birth, contract, organisation and one of the five assessment types; the assessment date decides its billing month. Time you log against an assessment is tracked separately on a non-billable hour type and never changes the fee.

Monthly invoice

Generate Invoice copies your Google Docs template once for every assessment in the selected month, across contracts and organisations. Generating again asks first, keeps the month’s invoice number, and only replaces the link after the new document is written. Logging time does not make an invoice out of date; adding, editing or deleting an assessment does.

BAS reporting

Actuals come from invoices, payments and recorded expenses. Forecasts come from timesheets and expense schedules. Submitted snapshots keep their accounting basis, calculation and source hash.
