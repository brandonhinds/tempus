This guide ships with the same build as the app, so its workflows match the version you are using.

Upgrade and backups

Tempus disables financial pages until the one-time upgrade has created and verified a spreadsheet copy. Failed migrations show their error and resume from the unfinished step.

Expenses and GST

Record purchases as transactions and payments. A recurring rule is only a forecast. Taxable GST is included in BAS actuals only after reconciliation, claimable-GST confirmation and receipt attachment.

Assessments

Turn on Lil Assessments mode to open Assessments at the current month. Record the client, date of birth, contract, organisation and one of the five assessment types; the assessment date decides its billing month. Time you log against an assessment is tracked separately on a non-billable hour type and never changes the fee.

Monthly invoice

Generate Invoice copies your Google Docs template once for every assessment in the selected month, across contracts and organisations. Generating again asks first, keeps the month’s invoice number, and only replaces the link after the new document is written. Logging time does not make an invoice out of date; adding, editing or deleting an assessment does.

BAS reporting

Actuals come from invoices, payments and reconciled expenses. Forecasts come from timesheets and expense rules. Submitted snapshots keep their accounting basis, calculation and source hash.
