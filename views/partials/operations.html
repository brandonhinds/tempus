Records a payment for the full amount. Turn off for a bill you haven't paid yet; it will show as Unpaid and won't count on a cash-basis BAS until you record the payment.

Upgrade and backups

Tempus disables financial pages until the one-time upgrade has created and verified a spreadsheet copy. Failed migrations show their error and resume from the unfinished step.

Expenses: recurring bills
  1. Open Expenses → Schedules and choose New schedule.
  2. Enter the vendor, the amount each time (including GST), the frequency and the start date, which is the first payment. Add an end date only if the bill stops. A bill on the 29th, 30th or 31st falls on the last day of a shorter month, then returns to its usual day.
  3. Choose Save schedule. The next 12 months of payments appear under Transactions → Due and upcoming.

Editing a schedule rebuilds its upcoming payments. Ones already recorded or paid are never changed. Turn off Active to pause a schedule, or use Stop schedule to end it today. Use Extend to 12 months ahead now and then so long-running schedules keep a year of payments listed.

Expenses: recording and paying

A scheduled bill: choose Mark paid to record it and its payment in one step. Use Record as unpaid bill if it has arrived but isn't paid yet. Use Skip if it won't happen this time.

A one-off purchase: choose Record expense. Leave Paid now on to record the payment at the same time. Turn it off for a bill you'll pay later.

Each expense shows Paid, Part-paid or Unpaid and the amount outstanding. Use Record payment to pay what's owing, including in instalments. Payments are listed under the expense with their reference.

Fixing mistakes: Remove payment undoes a payment, and Edit corrects the details. Void keeps the expense for the record but leaves it out of every total. A reconciled expense is locked, so void it and record a correction instead.

Expenses and GST

On a cash basis an expense counts when it is paid, so an unpaid expense counts as $0. On an accrual basis it counts on its invoice or purchase date. A GST credit (BAS 1B) counts only after you add a receipt and Reconcile the expense with the GST credit claimed. Upcoming schedule payments are forecasts, never actuals. The dashboard, the BAS table and the BAS calculation all read company expenses from the Expenses page. The Deductions page is for salary sacrifice and extra super only.

Assessments

Turn on Lil Assessments mode to open Assessments at the current month. Record the client, date of birth, contract, organisation and one of the five assessment types; the assessment date decides its billing month. Time you log against an assessment is tracked separately on a non-billable hour type and never changes the fee.

Monthly invoice

Generate Invoice copies your Google Docs template once for every assessment in the selected month, across contracts and organisations. Generating again asks first, keeps the month’s invoice number, and only replaces the link after the new document is written. Logging time does not make an invoice out of date; adding, editing or deleting an assessment does.

BAS reporting

Actuals come from invoices, payments and reconciled expenses. Forecasts come from timesheets and expense schedules. Submitted snapshots keep their accounting basis, calculation and source hash.
