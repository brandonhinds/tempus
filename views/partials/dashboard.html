Xero Payroll Helper

Using the Xero helper

You're always in control

  • This helper runs entirely inside your own browser, on the Xero account you're already signed into.
  • Tempus has no connection to Xero — no password, no login, no API key, and no access to your Xero account or anyone else's.
  • The daily-totals script only types your hours into the timesheet boxes; nothing is recorded until you press Save Draft.
  • The sessions script writes each day's start, end and break times into your draft timesheet — the same effect as typing them yourself (that timesheet type saves as you go). The draft stays fully editable.
  • Neither script ever submits — sending the timesheet to your employer is always your click.
  • If the timesheet you paste into doesn't match the month you copied, the script stops and says so — it can't fill the wrong month.

Step by step

  1. Sign in to Xero as you normally would.
  2. Go to Payroll → Xero Me → Timesheets.
  3. Open the timesheet for the month you're copying. If it doesn't exist yet, click + Add Timesheet, choose the period and the matching type (see below), then Continue.
  4. In Tempus, open this Xero Payroll Helper, check the month pills in the title bar (early in a new month it pre-selects the month just gone) and the contract if you have several, then click “Copy … for Xero” — the button names exactly what it copies.
  5. Back in Xero, open the developer console: press F12 (Windows) or ⌘+⌥+I (Mac), then click the Console tab.
  6. Click into the console, paste (Ctrl/⌘ + V) and press Enter.
  7. The helper fills your time and shows a summary. Check every day against Tempus.
  8. Daily totals: press Save Draft and confirm the Monthly Total. Sessions: refresh the page to see the times. When you're happy, press Submit.

Pick the timesheet type that matches the helper view

When you create the month's timesheet, Xero asks how you want to enter time — and it must match what you're copying. If the helper is showing the daily-totals grid, choose Total hours worked (the script fills the Ordinary Hours line). If it's showing the sessions view (a contract with start & end times), choose Start & end hours (the script writes each day's sessions and breaks). Paste into the wrong type and the script stops without changing anything.

Welcome to Tempus

You're all set to start tracking your time and income. Here are some suggestions on what to do to get going:

  • Create a contract to set your rates and dates. You cannot add time entries without this.
  • Explore Settings and feature flags (you can toggle them on or off any time without breaking anything).
  • Check the About tab for updates.
  • Add your first time entries directly from the calendar.

Need help? Use the "Docs" link for full guidance.

Tempus is also under constant, active development, so if you find bugs or feature requests either raise an issue in the GitHub repo or contact Brandon directly.
