## Design Context

### Users

Tempus serves independent professionals and small-business operators who need to capture work quickly, then turn it into reliable invoices, expense records, payroll estimates and BAS evidence. They use it in short mobile moments during the day and in focused desktop reconciliation sessions.

### Brand Personality

Calm, exact and quietly capable. The interface should build confidence around consequential financial work without feeling institutional or visually noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the coworker fork as the definitive visual language: restrained, high-contrast productivity surfaces; the existing green accent; considered light and dark themes; and the established Tempus typographic identity. New financial workflows should feel native to the fork, use progressive disclosure, and avoid generic dashboard card grids.

### Design Principles

- Make record state, provenance and reconciliation status immediately legible.
- Keep frequent time-entry actions fast on touch and keyboard, while revealing advanced financial detail only when needed.
- Treat destructive or irreversible transitions as explicit lifecycle actions with plain-language consequences.
- Preserve full functionality on mobile and meet accessible contrast, focus, reduced-motion and semantic-navigation expectations.
- Prefer one authoritative view of each fact; distinguish actuals, forecasts and unresolved evidence rather than blending them.
